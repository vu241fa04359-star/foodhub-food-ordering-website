About Space Info

Why we keep these notes

Space is enormous and mostly silent, but the small facts about it are worth collecting. This page is our shelf of them — planets we can name, missions we still talk about, and galaxies too far to visit but not too far to understand.

A short history of leaving Earth

1957

Sputnik 1

The first artificial satellite, launched by the Soviet Union, opens the space age.

1969

Apollo 11

Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin become the first humans to walk on the Moon.

1977

Voyager launches

Twin probes begin a journey that will carry them beyond the solar system.

1990

Hubble deployed

A space telescope begins returning some of the clearest images ever taken of deep space.

2021

James Webb launches

The largest space telescope built extends our view further back in time.
